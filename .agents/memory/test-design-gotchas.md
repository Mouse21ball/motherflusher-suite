## Box Chevy — best-5-from-10 low evaluation wipes out "pair" advantage
The low evaluator picks the optimal 5-card ace-to-five low from ALL available cards. A hand like A-A-2-3-4-5 can always pick A-2-3-4-5 (wheel) by dropping one Ace — same as a pure A-2-3-4-5 hand. Use cards with no low ranks at all (e.g. K-Q-J-10-9-8) as the "weak low" comparator.


**Why:** Earlier lowball tests expected extra paired ranks to worsen a hand even when best-five selection could discard those ranks.

**How to apply:** Use exactly five-card snapshots when testing paired lowball categories. Test the separate ten-card made-hand rule independently.
