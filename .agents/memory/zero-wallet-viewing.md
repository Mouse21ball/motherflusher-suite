---
name: Zero-wallet viewing
description: Product rules for watching live tables without a playable wallet.
---
Zero-chip PLAY must mean real watch-only access, not seating, automatic borrowing, a free grant, or a simulated game. Show live table action with a persistent “Watching only — get chips to play” indication and keep lobby navigation independent of the connection.

**Why:** The user wants broke players to see the games before deciding whether to get chips, without joining hands or making chip-dependent interactions.

**How to apply:** Enforce the viewing boundary on the server as well as in controls. Preserve viewer intent on reconnect; subsequent wallet funding does not itself authorize automatic seating. Spectators must not start or fund a game.
