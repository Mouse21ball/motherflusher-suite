The user states: “personal consumable chip packs are not currently approved for sale.”

**Why:** The four chip-pack examples in the purple/gold redesign are visual inspiration, not authorization to introduce purchase products.

**How to apply:** In visual work, do not create, register or wire new consumable chip-pack SKUs. Preserve existing approved purchase flows and products; do not offer one displayed amount/price while purchasing a different product.
